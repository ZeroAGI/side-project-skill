## 6. a16z thesis: 'the hard problem moves from how do I build it to what do I build'

- **类型**：trend
- **来源**：<https://a16z.com/notes-on-ai-apps-in-2026/>
- **域名**：`a16z.com`
- **日期**：2026-01-08
- **热度**：AI coding startups generated $1B+ in new revenue in 2025 alone; enterprises with 3+ model families in production grew from 68% to 81%

In 'Notes on AI Apps in 2026,' a16z partner Anish Acharya argues that as agents reliably handle execution, the central knowledge-work challenge shifts from building to ideation and prioritization. He identifies a structural gap: all current tools are for making, not for thinking. AI models produce bland, derivative product ideas. He identifies four durable app advantages that prevent foundation models from subsuming vertical apps: multi-model domains, cornered data resources, network products, and large feature surfaces. He also identifies service functions (legal, finance, HR) as the largest software-displacement opportunity.
